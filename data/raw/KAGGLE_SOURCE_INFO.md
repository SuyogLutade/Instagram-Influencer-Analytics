## Included Columns & Data Dictionary
1. `rank`: Global standing rank from original source (1 to 200).
2. `channel_info`: Account handle / username (e.g., `cristiano`, `kyliejenner`, `leomessi`).
3. `influence_score`: Evaluated impact / clout score (out of 100).
4. `posts`: Cumulative posts published by the account.
5. `followers`: Total follower count.
6. `avg_likes`: Average likes received per post.
7. `60_day_eng_rate`: 60-day engagement rate recorded as decimal fraction (e.g. 0.0139 = 1.39%).
8. `new_post_avg_like`: Average likes on recently published posts.
9. `total_likes`: Cumulative likes across account history.
10. `country`: Primary country or base region of the creator/entity.
